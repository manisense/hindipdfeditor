# Keyword Universe and Search Demand for a Hindi PDF Editor (India, 2026)

Research date: 2026-09-25. Method note: live Google Autocomplete (suggestqueries.google.com), Google Trends, Ahrefs, Semrush, Similarweb, UPDF, Adobe Community, YourStory and DocSet pages were all **blocked by this environment's egress proxy**. So the evidence below comes from web-search result pages (titles, URLs, and search-engine summaries of third-party tool data), not from first-hand tool exports. **No keyword-level volume in this file was measured directly.** Where a number appears, it is quoted from a search snippet summarising Ahrefs, Semrush or Similarweb data and is labelled that way. Every volume tier in the Inferences sections is the researcher's own estimate and is labelled as such.

## What are the main head and long-tail queries (English / Hinglish / Devanagari)?

### Takeaway
The keyword space splits in two. First, a small, underserved **Hindi-specific core**: edit Hindi text in a PDF, broken matras or garbled copy-paste, Kruti Dev/Devlys to Unicode, Hindi OCR, and Hindi PDF to Word. Second, a very large, highly competitive **generic utility shell**: compress, merge, split, jpg to pdf, and sarkari "resize to X KB". Hinglish "kaise kare" queries are real and are mostly answered by YouTube videos and small Hindi blogs. Devanagari queries are mostly answered by the big tools' `/hi/` localized pages.

## What search volumes / trend signals are available?

### Takeaway
Almost no public keyword-level India volumes exist for the Hindi-specific terms. The only quoted figures cover the generic "compress pdf" head term and traffic for large competitors, and even those conflict. Treat everything as directional until checked in Google Keyword Planner or Google Search Console.

### Cited Findings
- A search-engine summary of Ahrefs data states that **"compress pdf" has a monthly search volume of 1.6M in India**, and that compress2go.com ranks #1 for it in India with about 124K monthly visits from the keyword. — [Ahrefs compress2go (via search snippet)](https://ahrefs.com/websites/compress2go.com). **Conflict:** another search summary, of Ahrefs' pdf.io page, calls the same 1.6M figure "globally". — [Ahrefs pdf.io (via search snippet)](https://ahrefs.com/websites/pdf.io). Neither page could be fetched to confirm.
- 11zon.com ranks #6 for "jpg to pdf" in India (about 400.6K monthly visits from it), #5 for "compress pdf" (about 260.5K), and #4 for "pdf compressor" (about 164.8K). Its core audience is in India. These figures come from a search summary of Ahrefs data. — [ahrefstop 11zon (via search snippet)](https://ahrefstop.com/websites/11zon.com). Note that a #5 position yielding 260K visits sits awkwardly with #1 compress2go at 124K. The underlying data may mix countries or dates. Treat with caution.
- iLovePDF: India drove about 48M visits in August 2026, 29.3% of the site's traffic (Similarweb, via search summary). Separately, about 47M of about 150M global visits came from India in October 2024, "surpassing Amazon.in". — [Similarweb ilovepdf](https://www.similarweb.com/website/ilovepdf.com/), [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2026/02/ilovepdf-beats-amazon-india-traffic) (neither fetched directly)

- Pi7.org's audience overlaps with 11zon.com, simpleimageresizer.com and imresizer.com. These are the direct competitors in India's sarkari compress/resize niche. — [Similarweb pi7 (via search snippet)](https://www.similarweb.com/website/pdf.pi7.org/)
- Keyword-volume searches for "kruti dev" India returned no published volume. — search returned only tool vendor pages, e.g. [ContentSERP India keyword tool](https://contentserp.in/keyword-research-tool-india/)

### Inferences
- India is the single largest market for free PDF utility sites (iLovePDF alone gets about 48M India visits a month). So generic PDF-tool demand in India is in the tens of millions of searches a month in total, but it is captured by very strong domains.
- The existence of dedicated per-size landing pages (50KB, 100KB, 200KB, 500KB) on Adobe `/in/`, Pi7, 11zon, Zamzar and Smallpdf strongly suggests each size variant carries meaningful standalone India volume. Researcher's estimate: tens of thousands a month for 100KB/200KB. This is unmeasured.
- Kruti Dev converters are sustained by exam and government typing culture. Many single-purpose exact-match domains (krutidev-to-unicode.com, krutidevconverter.in), plus a government-hosted converter (updes.up.nic.in), point to steady, evergreen demand.

## Who ranks today, and where are the weak SERPs a small site can win?

### Takeaway
Generic utility SERPs are locked up by iLovePDF, Smallpdf, Adobe, PDF24, Sejda, 11zon and Pi7. **Hindi-specific problem SERPs** (broken matras, garbled copy-paste, editing an existing Hindi text line, Hindi PDF to Word without font loss) are weak. They are filled by Quora, Adobe Community threads, YouTube, thin listicles from global vendors (UPDF, Wondershare, PDFgear, SignNow), and tiny Indian tool sites. None of these actually solve Devanagari shaping.

### Cited Findings
- Hindi-language editing SERPs are dominated by localized `/hi/` pages from global tools: Smallpdf, iLovePDF, PDF24, Adobe `in_hi`, HiPDF and Sejda. — [Smallpdf hi](https://smallpdf.com/hi/edit-pdf), [PDF24 hi](https://tools.pdf24.org/hi/edit-pdf), [HiPDF hi](https://www.hipdf.com/hi/pdf-editor)
- "hindi pdf editor" style pages from global vendors are generic editors with a Hindi label. SignNow describes "text editing, form filling, annotation… merge or split". The HiPDF, PDF2Go and PDFzorro descriptions talk about "adding text, images, shapes", not about correcting Hindi shaping. — [SignNow](https://www.signnow.com/features/online-pdf-editor-for-hindi-free), [PDF2Go](https://www.pdf2go.com/edit-pdf)
- Forum and UGC results rank for the problem queries: Adobe Community (the matra-scatter thread; the Mangal export thread; the Hindi PDF to Word garbled thread) and Quora ("How to copy the Hindi text from PDF when it is displaying garbage…"; "Hindi PDF … convert it into a word file with Hindi text properly"). — [Adobe Community](https://community.adobe.com/t5/Acrobat/Converting-Hindi-pdf-to-Word-generates-garbled-text-any-help/m-p/10560461), [Quora](https://www.quora.com/I-have-a-Hindi-PDF-file-how-can-I-convert-it-into-a-word-file-with-Hindi-text-properly-online-and-free)
- For garbled copy-paste, one explanatory article notes that legacy fonts (Kruti Dev, Shivaji01, Chanakya) map "क" to "d", "भ" to "B", and so on. It recommends converters such as Font2Unicode or Devlys to Unicode. — [Digital Orientalist](https://digitalorientalist.com/2025/12/02/why-extracting-hindi-text-from-pdfs-is-so-much-harder-than-english-and-how-you-can-do-it/)
- Kruti Dev converter SERPs are served by typing/utility sites (typingbaba, indiatyping, techwelkin, rajbhasha.net, converterplanet), exact-match domains, a Softonic Android APK listing, and a UP government converter. — [typingbaba](https://www.typingbaba.com/converter/krutidev-to-unicode-converter.php), [techwelkin](https://techwelkin.com/tools/krutidev-unicode-krutidev-font-converter/), [UP govt](https://updes.up.nic.in/esd/font_converter/index.html), [Softonic](https://kruti-dev-to-unicode-converter.en.softonic.com/android)
- Hindi OCR SERPs are served mostly by small independent tools (i2OCR, fastocr, clevertools.in, typeinhindi, rajtool, myfreeocr), several of which advertise in-browser/WebAssembly processing. — [RajTool](https://www.rajtool.com/pdf-ocr), [FastOCR](https://fastocr.org/hindi-ocr)
- The sarkari resizer niche already has many exam-specific micro-sites that pitch "no upload / 100% private", e.g. ezssc.in ("Works 100% in browser") and ExamMint ("100% Free & Private"). — [ezssc](https://ezssc.in/image-cropper/), [ExamMint](https://resizer.exammint.in/ssc-cgl/)
- Play Store "PDF text editor" apps are generic, not Hindi-specific. The project's own GitHub repo (manisense/hindipdfeditor) already surfaces for a "hindi pdf editor app" query. — [Play Store PDF Text Editor](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.arzdrz.pdf_text_editor&hl=hi), [GitHub](https://github.com/manisense/hindipdfeditor)

### Inferences
- **Winnable gaps for a small new site, strongest first:**
  1. **Broken matra / garbled Hindi problem queries** (English, Hinglish, Devanagari). UGC and forum results rank here, which is the classic sign of a weak SERP, and the product's core differentiator (correct shaping via HTML render) is exactly the fix.
  2. **"Hindi PDF editor" comparison and alternative queries.** Only thin vendor listicles compete (UPDF, SignNow). An honest page showing matra rendering before and after, with screenshots, could outrank them.
  3. **"Kruti Dev PDF" to Unicode or edit** (PDF-specific, rather than the text-paste converters that dominate "kruti dev to unicode"). Existing converters handle pasted text, not PDFs. Per AGENTS.md, the product does not decode legacy fonts; it offers raster-only Unicode replacement. Page copy must say this honestly (for example, "edit a Kruti Dev PDF by replacing text with Unicode") and must not promise conversion.
  4. **Hinglish how-to long tail** ("pdf me hindi kaise likhe", "hindi pdf edit kaise kare"). Current results are YouTube and thin blogs that drift to "pdf kaise banaye".
  5. **Hindi OCR / Hindi PDF to Word:** medium difficulty, with fragmented small competitors.
- Hard, long-term only: generic "compress pdf", "merge pdf", "jpg to pdf". Size-specific sarkari variants are medium to hard: Pi7, 11zon and Adobe `/in/` already have dedicated pages, and a long tail of exam micro-sites exists.

### Gaps
- Actual top-10 positions per keyword for India (gl=in) could not be verified. The US-based search tool and blocked fetches mean the SERP compositions above are approximate for India.
- No backlink or Domain Rating figures for the small competitors (ezssc, rajtool, clevertools, krutidev-to-unicode.com) could be retrieved.

## Which queries trigger AI Overviews, video carousels, or PAA in India?

### Takeaway
AI Overviews have been live in India in Hindi since August 2024, and AI Mode added Hindi in September 2025. So informational "kaise kare / कैसे करें" queries are likely to show AI answers. Query-level trigger data could not be observed.

### Cited Findings
- Google introduced Hindi AI Overviews in India in August 2024 and expanded AI Overviews to 100+ countries in October 2024. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/08/27/googles-ai-overviews-in-hindi-need-a-quality-upgrade), [Croma](https://www.croma.com/unboxed/google-ai-overviews-is-coming-to-india)
- AI Mode added Hindi (among five new languages) from September 2025. Search Live launched in India in English and Hindi, and AI Mode expanded to seven more Indian languages (October 2025). — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-expands-ai-mode-beyond-english-461680), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/08/googles-search-live-comes-to-india-ai-mode-gets-more-languages)
- TechCrunch found many Hindi AI Overview answers inaccurate in 2024. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/08/27/googles-ai-overviews-in-hindi-need-a-quality-upgrade)
- YouTube videos rank in the main results for Hinglish "pdf me … kaise likhe/type kare mobile se" queries. — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ytRi2v0IIBM), [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r6O7lOEsgo4)

### Inferences
- "kaise kare / कैसे करें" how-to queries in Hinglish and Devanagari are the most exposed to AI Overviews and video carousels. Their pages should be built with a short how-to video (YouTube, Hindi voice-over), step-by-step HowTo-style content, and a clear tool CTA, so the site gets cited or clicked from the AI answer or video pack.
- Transactional tool queries ("compress pdf to 100kb", "kruti dev to unicode") usually show tool results rather than AI Overviews (general SERP pattern; not verified for these queries).

### Gaps
- It could not be verified which specific queries trigger AI Overviews, video carousels or PAA on google.co.in. This needs a manual check from an Indian IP or a rank tracker with India SERP features.

## Recommended clusters, priority order, and language targeting

### Takeaway
Lead with the Hindi-specific problem and editor clusters, where competition is weak and product fit is unique. Add Hinglish how-to content with video. Treat sarkari compress/resize as a medium-term traffic engine, and generic PDF utilities as long-term only.

### Cited Findings
- The major tools already localize every utility in Hindi (`/hi/edit-pdf`, `/hi/merge_pdf`, `/hi/ocr-pdf`, `in_hi` pages). - Privacy and in-browser processing is already a selling point used by sarkari resizer and OCR competitors. — [ezssc](https://ezssc.in/image-cropper/), [RajTool](https://www.rajtool.com/pdf-ocr)
