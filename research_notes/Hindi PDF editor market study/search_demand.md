# India search demand for Hindi/English PDF and document tasks (2025–2026)

Method note: All volumes below are **Semrush estimates, India database**, read from Semrush's free public "website overview" pages (which show the top 5 organic keywords of a domain, with volume). Unless marked otherwise they are **October 2026 data, pages updated ~11 Nov 2026, fetched 26 Nov 2026**. Semrush volumes are modelled estimates, not Google-measured counts; treat them as order-of-magnitude. Google Keyword Planner, Google Trends (India) and the project's own Search Console were **not** accessed in this session. Items with no number are ranked by reasoning and are marked "estimated".

## Which task clusters have the highest volume in India?

### Takeaway
Generic English PDF utilities are an order of magnitude larger than everything else: "jpg to pdf" ~7.5M/month and "pdf to word" ~5M/month in India, versus ~10K–250K for the Hindi font-conversion cluster. But the Hindi font-conversion cluster (Kruti Dev / Mangal / DevLys / Shree Lipi / AMS converters) is far bigger than "Hindi PDF edit" itself, and is served by small single-purpose sites, which makes it the largest *winnable* Hindi-specific cluster.

### Inferences
Estimated ranking of the requested clusters by India monthly volume (tiers; numbers only where measured above):
1. **Tier A, millions/month (measured):** jpg to pdf (~7.5M), pdf to word (~5M), merge pdf (~3.35M). "Edit pdf" generally and "compress pdf" are very likely in the same tier (no India figure visible this session; earlier report cited an unclear-geo 1.6M for "compress pdf").
2. **Tier B, hundreds of thousands (estimated):** size-target compression (pdf under 100/200/500kb) and exam photo/signature resize ("photo 20kb", "50kb", "signature resize") — inferred from 11zon and pi7 each drawing 5–10M visits/month mostly on these tasks, from 11zon's top keywords, and from Adobe building per-size landing pages. Hindi typing (InScript/Remington/Google Input) likely also here: typing sites draw 0.5–1.8M visits/month each, though their top keywords are translation and brand terms.
3. **Tier B/C, ~10K–250K (measured):** legacy font conversion as a cluster — Mangal↔Kruti 135K, Kruti→Mangal 60.5K, AMS 60.5K, krutidev10→unicode 49.5K, DevLys→unicode 40.5K, unicode→krutidev 33.1K, Shree Lipi 22.2K, plus possibly ~246K for "krutidev to unicode" (unverified). Summed head terms plausibly exceed ~400K/month. Chanakya↔Unicode is real but smaller (estimated low tens of thousands at most; no figure).
4. **Tier C (estimated):** translate pdf english to hindi, pdf to word hindi, hindi OCR / image to hindi text, scan/camera to pdf (camera scanning mostly happens in apps, not search), affidavit / application format hindi (large informational demand historically, but no figure found).
5. **Tier D (estimated, smallest):** "hindi pdf edit" / "हिंदी PDF एडिट" and broken-matra problem queries — specific, high intent, but far smaller than conversion or generic queries (consistent with the earlier report's Low–Med estimate).
- The legacy-font cluster is dominated by Hinglish/English Latin-script phrasing ("mangal to kruti", "kruti dev to unicode"), not Devanagari, and spelling variants (kurtidev, krutidev10) carry real volume — a page must target them.
- Every visible Hindi-font keyword has CPC $0 in Semrush, implying little paid competition and low ad monetisation value; the value is user acquisition, not ads.

### Gaps

## Seasonality (exam form windows, admission season)

### Takeaway
Only indirect evidence: traffic to the two biggest exam-upload utility sites fell 30–45% from February to April 2025, and Hindi-converter traffic swings 25–65% month to month. No direct Google Trends series was retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- 11zon.com visits: Feb 2025 10.23M, Mar 6.93M, Apr 5.57M — [Semrush via search snippet](https://cw.semrush.com/website/11zon.com/overview)
- pi7.org visits: Feb 2025 10.4M, Mar 8.06M — [Semrush via search snippet](https://tr.semrush.com/website/pi7.org/overview)
- krutidevunicode.com visits: Aug 2026 63.5K, Sep 57.4K, Oct 19K (−67% MoM) — [Semrush](https://ja.semrush.com/website/krutidevunicode.com/overview)
- hindityping.info: Aug 593.6K, Sep 716K, Oct 2026 549.9K — [Semrush](https://semrush.com/website/hindityping.info/overview)
- rajbhasha.net: Oct 2026 153.4K, +25% vs Sep — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/rajbhasha.net/overview/)
- indiatyping.com: Aug 1.91M, Sep 2.5M, Oct 2026 1.8M — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/indiatyping.com/overview/)

### Inferences
- Size-target compression and photo resize are likely seasonal around recruitment/admission form windows (the Feb peak in 2025 fits board-exam and application seasons), but one quarter of data cannot separate seasonality from ranking changes.
- September peaks for Hindi typing/conversion sites coincide with Hindi Diwas (14 September) and "Hindi Pakhwada" in government offices, a plausible driver for Rajbhasha-related conversion — unverified hypothesis.
- October dips may reflect the festival season (Navratri/Dussehra/Diwali) reducing office/form work — hypothesis.

### Gaps
- No Google Trends weekly series for "photo resize 20kb", "compress pdf 100kb" or "kruti dev to unicode" in India. Exam calendars (SSC, UPSC, RRB, state PSC) were not cross-referenced with traffic data in this session.

## SERP strength: weak (forums/small sites) vs dominated by iLovePDF/Smallpdf/Adobe

### Takeaway
Generic utilities are locked by iLovePDF (#1) and Smallpdf (#2–3) plus Adobe's India size pages. The Hindi font-conversion SERPs are held by small, low-authority single-purpose sites (Authority Score 10–42, blogspot converters), and even those rank #5–8 for the biggest terms — a weak SERP with real volume.

### Cited Findings
- iLovePDF ranks #1 India for jpg to pdf, pdf to word, pdf to jpg; Smallpdf #2–3 for those plus merge pdf and word to pdf — [Semrush: ilovepdf.com](https://www.semrush.com/website/ilovepdf.com/overview/); [Semrush: smallpdf.com](https://www.semrush.com/website/smallpdf.com/overview/)
- Adobe India has per-size compression pages (100KB, 150KB, 250KB) — [Adobe IN](https://www.adobe.com/in/acrobat/online/compress-pdf-250kb.html)
- krutidevunicode.com: Authority Score 10, ranks #7 for "unicode to krutidev converter" (33.1K) and #6 for "krutidev unicode" — [Semrush](https://ja.semrush.com/website/krutidevunicode.com/overview)
- rajbhasha.net ranks #8 for "mangal to kruti" (135K), #8 for "kruti dev to mangal", #5 for "kurtidev10 to unicode" — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/rajbhasha.net/overview/)
- hindityping.info (Authority Score 42) ranks #1 for "devlys to unicode" and "shree lipi converter", #2 for "ams font converter", #7 for "mangal to kruti" — [Semrush](https://semrush.com/website/hindityping.info/overview)
- Blogspot sites exist as dedicated Chanakya↔Unicode converters — [Semrush](https://tr.semrush.com/website/chanakya-to-unicode.blogspot.com/overview)
- (From earlier SEO report, not re-checked) "Hindi PDF editor" SERPs are held by vendor listicles (UPDF) and programmatic pages (SignNow); broken-matra queries are answered by Quora/Adobe Community — see `reports/Hindi PDF editor SEO strategy.md`.

### Inferences
- Least served relative to size: (1) legacy-font conversion (Mangal↔Kruti, DevLys, Chanakya, Kruti Dev PDF) — 100K+ volumes held by AS 10–42 sites; (2) Hindi-specific PDF tasks (Hindi PDF edit, Hindi OCR, Hindi PDF to Word without broken font) — small volume, thin SERPs.
- Largest but most contested: generic utilities and size-target compression — winnable only at the long tail ("pdf 200kb hindi me", exam-specific pages), not head terms.
- No existing converter targets "Kruti Dev **PDF**" end-to-end (convert a legacy PDF to Unicode while keeping layout); that matches the product's web KrutiDev decoding and is a defensible wedge — inference, SERP not checked this session.

### Gaps
- Direct SERP composition for "photo resize 20kb", "affidavit format hindi", "application format hindi", "hindi ocr", "translate pdf english to hindi" was not observed in this session.

## Growth in Hindi-language and voice search; share of users preferring Indic languages

### Takeaway
Nearly all Indian internet users (98%, 870M in 2024) access some content in Indic languages and 57% of urban users prefer vernacular content; India's base hit ~958M in 2025. About a third of Google queries in India are spoken (2023). Hindi-specific voice-growth figures are old (2015–2019).

### Cited Findings
- IAMAI–Kantar Internet in India 2024: 870M users (98%) accessed the internet in Indic languages; 57% of urban users prefer vernacular content; 13% of offline people cite lack of local-language content — [Business Standard, Jan 2025](https://business-standard.com/india-news/india-s-internet-users-to-exceed-900-mn-in-2025-driven-by-indic-languages-125011600835_1.html); [Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/news-and-trends/internet-users-in-india-set-to-cross-900-mn-led-by-indic/485634)
- IAMAI–Kantar 2025 edition (Jan 2026): ~958M active users, +8% YoY; rural ~548M, more than half — [YourStory, Jan 2026](https://yourstory.com/2026/01/indias-internet-user-base-crosses-950-million-2025-iamai-report); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/news/story/indias-internet-user-base-tops-950-million-as-rural-growth-ai-drive-adoption-513353-2026-01-29)
- Google's Prabhakar Raghavan (June 2023): "In India, about a third of our search queries are spoken," vs ~3–4% in the US — [Inc42, 22 Jun 2023](https://inc42.com/?p=403119)
- Google (2019): local-language share of searches rose from 2% (2014) to 20% (2019); 90% of new users non-English — [Republic World](https://www.republicworld.com/india/90-percent-of-new-net-users-20-percent-searches-in-local-languages-google/)
- Hindi voice searches grew 400% YoY (2015–16); all-India voice search +270% YoY (2018) — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/bhopal/hindi-web-consumption-grows-at-over-90-so-does-google-services); [YourStory 2019](https://yourstory.com/2019/05/google-search-report-2018-voice-assistants-india)
- Google–KPMG (2017): 234M Indian-language users vs 175M English (end 2016); ~68–70% consider local-language content more reliable; 88% more likely to respond to local-language ads — [KPMG report](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/in/pdf/2017/04/Indian-languages-Defining-Indias-Internet.pdf); [Quartz](https://qz.com/india/972844/indias-internet-users-have-more-faith-in-content-thats-not-in-english-study-says)

### Inferences
- Despite Indic preference, PDF-task searches are overwhelmingly typed in English/Latin script (every measured keyword above is Latin). Hindi-preferring users appear to search tools in English or Hinglish and want Hindi *output*. Devanagari pages help with "how to" and voice-style queries rather than head tool terms.
- High voice share suggests natural-language Hindi/Hinglish questions ("pdf ka size kaise kam kare") are worth FAQ coverage.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 Hindi-specific search-share or voice-search figure found; the IAMAI 2025 coverage gives no language breakdown. KPMG figures are from 2017 and cited via the primary PDF and press, not re-verified page by page.
