# India-specific and Hindi-specific competitors to hindipdfeditor.com (as of late Nov 2026)

Method note: research done 2026-11-26 using web search and page fetches. Google Play pages and AppBrain refused direct fetches (truncated or connection refused), so the Play Store numbers below come from search-result snippets of the Play listings. They are snapshots of unknown date. Treat them as approximate and re-check them live before quoting. No Similarweb data for the niche sites was publicly reachable.

## Which Play Store apps rank for "Hindi PDF editor", "PDF editor Hindi", "Kruti Dev to Unicode", "Hindi OCR"? Installs, ratings, common 1–2 star complaints

### Takeaway
No large Hindi-specific PDF editor exists on Play. A search for "Hindi PDF editor" surfaces the owner's own app (100+ downloads) and then generic global PDF editors with 10M+ installs that do not position on Hindi. The KrutiDev converter and Hindi OCR niches are filled by small indie apps (about 10K installs each). Generic OCR apps with Hindi among 100+ languages are much bigger (1M to 10M+ installs).

### Cited Findings
**"Hindi PDF editor" / "PDF editor Hindi"**
- Hindi PDF Editor (com.hindipdfeditor.app, Pruning Labs, the owner's app): 100+ downloads, contains ads. The pitch is edit text, merge files and sign. No rating was visible in the snippet. — [Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hindipdfeditor.app&hl=en_IN)
- PDF Editor: Edit PDF, Sign PDF (xsdev): about 4.3 stars from about 132K reviews, 10M+ downloads. Recent complaints are about paywalls and crashes. — [Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details/PDF_text_editor_Edit_PDF?id=com.xsdev.pdfreader.pdfeditor.pdf.document&hl=en_US)
- PDF Editor – Edit Everything! (Desygner): about 4.4 stars from about 185K reviews, 10M+ downloads. The common complaints are forced subscriptions and export restrictions. — [Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details/PDF_Editor_Edit_Everything?id=com.desygner.pdf&hl=en_US)
- PDF Editor App (com.pdf.editorapp): 4.1 stars from about 2.69K reviews, 100K+ downloads. A recent complaint names subscription issues and unresponsive support. — [Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pdf.editorapp&hl=en_IN)
- PDF Editor: Edit, Sign & Scan (edit.pdf): 4.5 stars from about 4.26K reviews, 100K+ downloads. Praised as free and offline. One complaint: no bold text. — [Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=edit.pdf&hl=en)
- RevPDF: 4.7 stars from about 593 reviews, 50K+ downloads. Find-and-replace fails on flattened PDFs. — [Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.revpdf.editor)

**"Kruti Dev to Unicode"**
- Kruti Dev to Unicode Converter (com.mt.krutidevconverter): offline, converts both ways for Kruti Dev 010, word and page count, no internet permission. A paid Pro tier adds Word and .txt files (the INR price was not visible). The font is not bundled. The listing was about 51 days old at the time of the snippet. — [Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mt.krutidevconverter&hl=en)
- kruti Dev Font Converter (Narendra Bairagi, in.narenbairagi.unicode.converter): converts both ways, updated Oct 21, 2025. — [Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.narenbairagi.unicode.converter&gl=US)
- Kruti Dev to Unicode (AppBuddy52, com.vini7152.krutidevtounicode): 10,000+ downloads (AppBrain also shows about 19K total), free, claims other legacy fonts too. — [AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/app/kruti-dev-to-unicode/com.vini7152.krutidevtounicode) (via search snippet; a direct fetch failed)
- Other KrutiDev apps from small developers (Proud Indian Studio, Shubham Vashisht, Ignite Soul, SHALK Softech) appear only on APK mirror sites. — [Softonic](https://kruti-dev-to-unicode-kruti-dev-010-conversion.en.softonic.com/android), [Softonic 2](https://krutidev-unicode-converter.en.softonic.com/android)

**"Hindi OCR"**
- Image to Text Hindi OCR (MAXSAM APPS, com.urva.textscannerhindi): about 2.6 stars from about 121 ratings, about 10K downloads, released 2019 (third-party mirror data, dated). — [APKtume](https://apktume.com/android/us/app/com.urva.textscannerhindi/), [AppsForWindowsPC](https://appsforwindowspc.com/download-image-to-text-hindi-ocr-for-pc-windows/)
- Text Scanner – Image to Text (image.to.text.ocr): about 4.3 stars from about 193K reviews, 10M+ downloads, 100+ languages with auto-detection. — [Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=image.to.text.ocr&hl=en_US)
- Image to Text Converter – OCR (Aculix): about 4.2 stars from about 26K reviews, 1M+ downloads, lists Hindi. — [Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=aculix.smart.text.recognizer)
- OCR Image to Text Scanner (Prepostseo): about 3.7 stars, 100K+ downloads. Reviews say scanning broke after updates. — [Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.prepost.imagetotext&hl=en)

### Inferences
- The search term "Hindi PDF editor" on Play is effectively uncontested by a Hindi-specialist app at scale. The real competition is generic editors that win on install base. Their main weakness is aggressive paywalls and subscriptions. Users also complain that existing text cannot be edited on flattened PDFs.
- KrutiDev converter apps are small text-paste utilities. None found handles PDFs.
- Hindi-specific OCR apps are low quality (2.6 stars). The mass market uses generic multi-language OCR apps or Google Lens.

### Gaps
- The Play Store search ranking order could not be captured; Play pages refused fetches. Live 1–2 star review text for each app was not retrieved, so the complaints above come from snippet summaries only.
- No Play listing found for a Chanakya converter app, a Hindi typing app, or an exam-photo-resizer app. Not searched for lack of budget.
- Install counts for the KrutiDev Play apps other than AppBuddy52 are unknown.

## Which websites dominate KrutiDev↔Unicode conversion, Chanakya/legacy fonts, Hindi typing, exam photo/signature resize, "PDF under 100KB/200KB"?

### Takeaway
The niche web tools are fragmented and SEO-driven. RajTool is the most complete legacy-font player: both directions, DevLys and Chanakya, DOCX upload, and Google Workspace and Microsoft add-ins. Typing practice sites (TypingBaba, IndiaTyping, HindiTyping.info) and exam-portal tool pages (onlineresult.in, docset.in, mysarkarinaukri.com) serve the sarkari-form crowd. Global brands Adobe and iLovePDF dominate "compress PDF to 100KB" in India by sheer scale.

### Cited Findings
**KrutiDev / Chanakya conversion**
- RajTool (rajtool.com):
  - Converts KrutiDev 010/020 to Unicode and back.
  - Has separate DevLys, Unicode-to-Chanakya and Unicode-to-Raj-Font converters.
  - Accepts DOCX and TXT uploads and offers a "KrutiDev PDF to Word" tool.
  - Runs client-side.
  - Advertises free unlimited conversions, with premium add-in pricing on separate pages (no INR shown).
  - Claims "100% accuracy", "reviewed by 200+ government typists" and "trusted by 1000+ typists". These are self-reported.
  - Sources: [RajTool converter](https://www.rajtool.com/krutidev-converter), [Capterra listing](https://www.capterra.com/p/10035792/RajTool/)
- RajTool's Google Docs/Sheets add-on "Unicode Converter" converts KrutiDev and DevLys to Unicode for a selection or a whole document. It is free with paid features, was updated May 24, 2026, and had only one review visible. — [Google Workspace Marketplace](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/app/unicode_converter/64787471443)
- RajTool also runs a "Hindi Word Editor Online" for typing and editing in KrutiDev or Unicode, and claims to fix legacy font issues with the letter Sha. — [RajTool live editor](https://www.rajtool.com/live-word-editor)
- docset.in markets a Hindi Word to PDF tool for government and court filing. It says the tool detects legacy encodings and embeds the font. — [docset.in](https://docset.in/pdf-tools/hindi-word-to-pdf/)

**Hindi typing (InScript/Remington)**
- TypingBaba: free Mangal-font typing tests with Inscript and Remington layouts, and an online Hindi keyboard. — [TypingBaba test](https://www.typingbaba.com/test/online-hindi-typing-test-mangal-font.php), [keyboard](https://www.typingbaba.com/keyboard/online-hindi-keyboard.php)
- IndiaTyping.com: tests for Krutidev/DevLys, Inscript and Remington Gail, plus an SSC typing exam. — [IndiaTyping](https://indiatyping.com/index.php/typing-test), [Remington Gail](https://indiatyping.com/index.php/typing-test/typing-exam-remington-gail)
- HindiTyping.info: Remington GAIL and InScript tests aimed at SSC, a certificate with a verifiable number, and an English-to-Hindi transliteration typing tool. — [HindiTyping.info](https://hindityping.info/online-hindi-typing-test-krutidev-remington-gail/)
- TypingMaster: a paid Hindi test module with InScript and Remington layouts and a free trial. — [TypingMaster](https://www.typingmaster.com/typing-tutor/hindi-typing-test.html)
- MySarkariNaukri: a Hindi typing test for sarkari practice. — [mysarkarinaukri](https://www.mysarkarinaukri.com/tools/typing/?lang=hi)

**Exam photo/signature resizers**
- onlineresult.in:
  - SSC CGL/CHSL/MTS preset: photo 200×230 px at 20–50 KB, signature 140×60 px at 10–20 KB.
  - Adds a name-and-date strip.
  - Client-side, with a Hindi UI option.
  - Source: [onlineresult.in](https://www.onlineresult.in/tools/exam-photo-resizer?preset=ssc)
- docset.in: per-exam resize pages (SSC CGL, CHSL) with AI face cropping and a name and date overlay. — [docset.in SSC CGL](https://docset.in/resize/ssc-cgl)
- Testbook (a large exam-prep edtech) has an exam photo crop/resize tool. It lists the SSC CGL photo at 200×300, which conflicts with onlineresult's 200×230. — [Testbook](https://testbook.com/exam-photo-crop-resize-tool)
- Other resizers:
  - simpleimageresizer.com (SSC CGL/CHSL pages, with a disclaimer that it cannot check eligibility): [simpleimageresizer](https://simpleimageresizer.com/ssc-cgl-photo-and-signature-resizer)
  - globaldrishti.in (browser canvas): [globaldrishti](https://globaldrishti.in/tools/photo-signature-resizer/)
  - Odoo-hosted "img master": [freetool.odoo.com](https://freetool.odoo.com/img-master)

**PDF under 100KB/200KB**
- Adobe Acrobat India runs dedicated landing pages for 100KB, 150KB, 200KB and 250KB compression. They are free with an account. — [Adobe 100KB](https://www.adobe.com/in/acrobat/online/compress-pdf-100kb.html), [Adobe 200KB](https://www.adobe.com/in/acrobat/online/compress-pdf-200kb.html)
- MySarkariNaukri PDF compressor: 50/100/200/500 KB targets for SSC, UPSC, IBPS, RRB and state PSC forms. It claims to run in the browser. — [mysarkarinaukri](https://www.mysarkarinaukri.com/tools/pdf-compressor/)
- Other size-target pages:
  - edesy.in (preset to 200KB): [edesy](https://edesy.in/tools/compress-pdf-to-200kb)

**Scale of generic Indian PDF traffic**
- iLovePDF: about 47M of about 150M global visits came from India in October 2024, which beat Amazon.in for that month. YourStory reported this (Feb 2026) citing Ahrefs and SimilarWeb. The data is from 2024 and the article could not be fetched (403), so this comes from the search snippet. — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2026/02/ilovepdf-beats-amazon-india-traffic)
- Ahrefs ranks ilovepdf.com first in India's computers-and-electronics category, with about 42M organic traffic (estimate). — [Ahrefs India](https://ahrefs.com/websites/india/computers-and-electronics)
- iLovePDF has a Hindi-language editor UI. — [iLovePDF hi](https://www.ilovepdf.com/hi/edit-pdf)

### Inferences
- The traffic leader for each task is a generalist (iLovePDF, Adobe) or a sarkari portal's free tool page. Niche sites win long-tail queries in Hindi and Hinglish through per-exam landing pages ("SSC CGL", "20kb"). A per-exam page pattern looks table-stakes for SEO.
- RajTool is the closest legacy-font competitor. It reaches into Word, Docs and Sheets through add-ins, but no source shows it converting legacy text inside a PDF while keeping the layout.
- Many of these tools now advertise "client-side, files never leave device", so privacy is no longer a differentiator on its own.

### Gaps
- No public Similarweb or Semrush numbers were found for rajtool.com, typingbaba.com, indiatyping.com, onlineresult.in or docset.in. Their traffic is unknown.
- Other well-known KrutiDev converter sites were not individually checked, for example unicodetokrutidev or Chanakya-to-Unicode sites. The search surfaced RajTool almost exclusively.
- INR prices for RajTool premium, TypingMaster Hindi and the Play converter Pro tiers were not found.

## Does any existing tool edit Hindi text inside a PDF correctly? Claims vs reality

### Takeaway
Several web tools claim Hindi support: IndiaPDF Editor, eofficetool, RVS and tool-web.dev. On inspection, their claims cover adding new Hindi text or running Hindi OCR. None found explicitly claims, with evidence, that it correctly replaces existing Devanagari text inside the PDF content stream. No independent test was found.

### Cited Findings
- IndiaPDF Editor (indiapdfeditor.com):
  - Type new text in Hindi and nine other Indian scripts, correct ₹ symbol, OCR for scanned PDFs (Hindi plus English) that makes them searchable.
  - Free, with Premium free for signed-in users until 31 January 2027. Limits: 250 MB, 50 premium files a day, 10 AI documents a day.
  - Claims "your files never leave your device" but also offers AI chat and translation, without explaining where that data goes.
  - The page does not say whether existing Hindi text can be edited, and names no operator.
  - Source: [IndiaPDF Editor](https://indiapdfeditor.com/)
- eofficetool claims it supports Hindi Devanagari and embeds the correct font in the downloaded PDF. — [eofficetool](https://www.eofficetool.com/pdf-editor)
- tool-web.dev Browser PDF Editor: OCR in English, Hindi and Gujarati, run in the browser. — [tool-web.dev](https://tool-web.dev/tools/pdf-editor)
- RVS Education's free PDF editor advertises "full Hindi text support", processed locally. — [rvseducation.in](https://www.rvseducation.in/p/free-pdf-editor-tool.html)
- UPDF's vendor roundup "4 Top Hindi PDF Editors in 2026" names PDF2Go, HiPDF and PDFzorro, and promotes UPDF itself. — [UPDF](https://updf.com/edit-pdf/hindi-pdf-editor/)
- PDNob markets PDF-to-Hindi-text OCR extraction, not in-place editing. — [PDNob](https://www.pdnob.com/pdf-to-hindi-text.html)
- Context:
  - Generic editors handle Devanagari one character at a time without a shaping engine, so matras detach or misplace. This claim comes from vendor or blog content surfaced in search, not from an independent test.
  - RevPDF reviewers report find-and-replace failing on flattened PDFs: [RevPDF](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.revpdf.editor)
- hindipdfeditor.com's own public pages describe browser-rendered replacement text. The search snippet described edited pages as being exported as images, which may be outdated against the current AGENTS.md description (text removed from the content stream, new words as images). — [hindipdfeditor.com/edit](https://hindipdfeditor.com/edit/)

### Inferences
- "Hindi support" in competitor copy usually means a Devanagari font for added text, or Hindi OCR. Replacing existing Hindi text, and converting KrutiDev PDFs in place, appear unclaimed by competitors.
- IndiaPDF Editor is the most direct web competitor by name and positioning. It has a broad toolset and a free premium tier until Jan 2027, likely to grow users before monetizing. Its unnamed operator and vague AI privacy story are weaknesses.

### Gaps
- None of these tools was tested hands-on with a real Hindi PDF. Claims are unverified.
- No independent reviews or comparisons of Hindi in-place editing were found.

## Indian startups and government tools that overlap (Bhashini, Sarvam, Google Lens)

### Takeaway
Sarvam (Akshar workbench plus the Vision OCR model, Feb 2026) and Bhashini (Hindi OCR from IIIT Hyderabad, CAG-BHASHINI Anuvaad document translation from Sep 2026) overlap on Hindi OCR and document translation. Both target developers, enterprises and government, not consumer PDF editing. Neither edits PDFs.

### Cited Findings
- Sarvam Akshar:
  - A document digitisation workbench on the 3B-parameter Sarvam Vision model, unveiled at the AI Impact Summit in Feb 2026 as one of 11 launches.
  - An agent loop flags uncertain text for human review.
  - Covers 22 Indian languages plus English.
  - Sources: [Sarvam Akshar](https://www.sarvam.ai/products/akshar), [Business Today, 2026-02-05](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/sarvam-ai-unveils-sarvam-vision-a-multilingual-document-intelligence-model-514873-2026-02-05), [Newsbytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/science/sarvam-akshar-offers-document-intelligence-in-22-indian-languages/story)
- Sarvam-reported Hindi score on its own Indic OCR bench: Sarvam Vision 95.91 vs Gemini 3 Pro 95.12. This is a company claim with no independent verification found. — [Analytics India Magazine](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-news/sarvam-vision-beats-gemini-gpt-on-indic-ocr-across-22-indian-languages)
- Sarvam document limits conflict across pages:
  - The Document AI API docs say a multi-page PDF can have at most 10 pages: [Sarvam docs](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api/api-guides-tutorials/document-intelligence/overview.md)
  - Another Sarvam page cites 300 MB and 500 pages: [Sarvam doc-agents](https://www.sarvam.ai/products/doc-agents.md)
- Sarvam later released Vision 2.1, reportedly on Sep 24, 2026, which conflicts with the docs' references to v1.5. — [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/unlisted-companies/sarvam-launches-vision-2-1-ai-model-for-documents-and-indian-languages)
- Sarvam has a PDF-to-Hindi translation API: parse, OCR where needed, then translate. — [Sarvam docs](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api/api-guides-tutorials/doc-translation/guides/translate-a-pdf)
- CAG-BHASHINI Anuvaad launched Sep 30, 2026 for translating official CAG and audit documents. It is an institutional tool with controlled access, not a consumer one. — [News on AIR](https://newsonair.gov.in/dibd-cag-launch-ai-enabled-cag-bhashini-anuvaad-for-document-translation/), [Indian Infrastructure](https://indianinfrastructure.com/2026/10/01/digital-india-bhashini-division-and-cag-launch-cag-bhashini-anuvaad-to-enable-ai-powered-multilingual-translation-of-official-documents/)
- Bhashini-funded Hindi OCR (IIIT Hyderabad consortium) was applied to Lok Sabha debates. The consortium built OCR for 13 Indian languages. — [IIIT-H ILOCR](https://ilocr.iiit.ac.in/case_study/loksabha)
- The Bhashini app added an OCR feature, designed for printed text (date unconfirmed). — [Analytics India Magazine](https://analyticsindiamag.com/bhashini-introduces-ocr-feature-in-its-app)
- Bodhan AI released four Indic models for OCR, translation and speech in Sep 2026. — [Analytics Vidhya](https://www.analyticsvidhya.com/blog/2026/09/bodhan-ai-indic-models/)

### Inferences
- Sarvam and Bhashini are more likely suppliers (OCR and translation backends) than direct competitors. The memory notes say the project already uses Sarvam behind a Worker. A consumer "Akshar-lite" would be a threat only if Sarvam builds a consumer PDF app, and no evidence of one was found.
- Google Lens is the default free Hindi OCR on Android, but it was not researched here.

### Gaps
- No usable source found on Google Lens Hindi OCR quality or usage numbers, or on Google Translate document translation for Hindi PDFs. Not searched within budget.
- Bhashini's public consumer app install counts were not checked.
- No Indian startup was found building a consumer Hindi PDF editor with funding or press coverage.
