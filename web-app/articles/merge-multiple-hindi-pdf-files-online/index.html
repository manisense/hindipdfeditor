How to Merge Hindi PDF Files Into One (in the Right Order, Without Uploading Them)

Many application portals give you one upload slot for "all documents". So the marksheet, caste certificate, domicile and affidavit have to become a single PDF, in the right order, and under the size limit. Here's how to do it without handing your documents to a random website.

By Manish · Updated 25 September 2026 · about 4 minutes to read

Short answer

Open Merge PDF, select your files in the order they should appear, check the list, and click Merge. The combined PDF downloads straight away. Files are merged in your browser, so nothing is uploaded, and Hindi text is copied as it is. If the result is over the portal's size limit, compress each file first, then merge.

Before you merge: get the order right

Whoever checks your application will read the file top to bottom. Follow the order the notification lists documents in. If there isn't one, use this common order:

  1. Application form or its printout
  2. Identity proof (Aadhaar)
  3. Educational certificates, oldest first
  4. Category, domicile and income certificates
  5. Affidavits and declarations

Rename files with a number first, like 1-form.pdf and 2-aadhaar.pdf. File pickers sort by name, so selecting them in order becomes easy.

How to merge

1. Select the files

Open Merge PDF and choose two or more PDFs. They open in your browser and aren't uploaded anywhere.

2. Check the list

Files are combined in the order shown. Use "Add PDFs" to append more, and "Remove" to drop one. To move a file, remove it and add it again at the right point.

3. Merge and check the result

Click Merge. Open the downloaded file and scroll through once: right order, no missing pages, no sideways scans.

Keeping it under the size limit

Merging doesn't shrink anything. Five 300 KB scans make a 1.5 MB file. If the portal wants 500 KB or less:

  • Compress each scan first, since big phone scans are usually the problem.
  • Drop pages nobody asked for, like blank backs and instruction pages, with Split PDF.
  • Rescan at 150–200 DPI in greyscale if a single document is still huge.

Why merging locally matters for these documents

The files people merge for applications are exactly the sensitive ones: Aadhaar, caste and income certificates, marksheets. Many free online mergers upload them to a server to do the job. Merging in your browser means the documents never leave your phone or computer.

Other questions people ask

Will merging change or break the Hindi text?

No. Pages are copied as they are, fonts included, so Hindi text looks and copies exactly as it did in the original files.

Can I reorder files after adding them?

Files are combined in the order shown. To move one, remove it and add it again at the point you want, or select files in the right order to begin with.

The merged file is too big for the portal. What now?

Compress each document first, then merge. Our PDF size guide explains how to get scans under 100–200 KB.
