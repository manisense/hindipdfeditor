How to Compress Large Scanned Hindi PDFs Without Quality Loss

Learn how to reduce scanned Hindi PDF file size below 100KB, 200KB, or 500KB for government job portals without making Hindi matras blurry.

Direct Answer: How to Compress Large Scanned Hindi PDFs Without Quality Loss

To compress a large scanned Hindi PDF, open hindipdfeditor.com/edit/?tool=compress. Choose your preferred compression mode (Balanced, Strong, or Maximum Compression), and click Compress PDF. The tool optimizes image streams and removes redundant metadata while keeping Devanagari text razor-sharp for portal uploads.

Meeting Sarkari Portal Upload Size Limits (100KB - 300KB)

Government job applications (UPSC, SSC, State PSCs) typically restrict document upload sizes to under 200KB. Naive online compressors often over-compress documents, turning Hindi matras and text into unreadable pixelated blur.

Hindi PDF Editor uses smart DPI scaling to balance file size reduction with maximum Devanagari text legibility.
