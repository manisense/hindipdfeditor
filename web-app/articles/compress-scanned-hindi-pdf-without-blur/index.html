How to Shrink a Scanned Hindi PDF Without Making the Text Unreadable

Devanagari suffers first when a scan is compressed too hard: the dot of the anusvara disappears, ि and ी blur into each other, and half-letters turn to smudges. The trick is to cut size where it doesn't cost legibility.

By Manish · Updated 25 September 2026 · about 4 minutes to read

Short answer

Most of the size comes from how the page was scanned, so fix that first: 150–200 DPI, greyscale, cropped to the paper. Then compress in small steps with a quality slider, and after each step zoom in on a line with matras and conjuncts. Stop one step before the dots and matras start to smudge.

Where the megabytes come from

A phone photo of a page is usually 2–4 MB: full colour, very high resolution, plus the table and shadows around the paper. None of that helps anyone read the page. Removing it costs no legibility at all, and that's why it's the first step.

  • Resolution: 150–200 DPI is plenty for printed Hindi. 300 DPI and above mostly adds file size.
  • Colour: greyscale keeps stamps and signatures readable at a fraction of the size. Pure black-and-white makes files smaller still, but can erase light stamps and thin matras. Check before you use it.
  • Crop: trim to the paper edge. Backgrounds and shadows compress badly.

Compress in steps, not in one jump

1. Start in the middle

Open Compress PDF, pick the file, and start with the quality slider around the middle. Each page is re-encoded as a JPEG at that quality, in your browser.

2. Check the size, then the text

Look at the downloaded file's size. Then zoom to 150–200% on the densest Hindi line you have, such as a name with a conjunct or a line of small print. Can you still tell ि from ी, and see the anusvara dot?

3. Adjust by one step

Too big and still sharp: lower the quality a little. Small enough but smudged: raise it one step, and cut size somewhere else instead, with fewer pages or a better rescan.

A quick legibility check before you upload

  • Names and numbers (roll number, date of birth, amounts) are fully readable.
  • Stamps and signatures are still visible.
  • The smallest print on the page, often the most important line, can be read at normal zoom.

Other questions people ask

Why does Hindi text blur faster than English when compressed?

Devanagari packs a lot of detail into a small space: matras, dots, half-letters and the headline (शिरोरेखा). JPEG compression smooths fine detail first, so those marks go before the overall letter shape does.

Does compressing make the PDF text unselectable?

With Hindi PDF Editor, yes. Pages are re-encoded as images, so any selectable text is lost. For scans that doesn't matter, since they were images already.

Is the file uploaded to compress it?

No. Compression runs in your browser and the file stays on your device.
