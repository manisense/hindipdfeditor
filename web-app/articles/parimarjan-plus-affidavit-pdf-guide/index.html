Parimarjan Plus affidavit PDF: official format and preparation

Find the official Bihar Parimarjan Plus format, prepare a correction request and review your PDF copy. Understand why editing a PDF does not change land records.

Product guide by Hindi PDF Editor · Reviewed 2026-10-01

Direct answer

Obtain the official format for your application, match the required fields against evidence and review the new PDF. The editor helps prepare an application; it does not correct the official Jamabandi record.

Find the official format

Bihar Bhumi’s Jamabandi FAQ points applicants to Parimarjan Plus for correction requests. Check the application type and official instructions for your case. Obtain the applicable format there; a generic sample is not an approved affidavit.

An application is different from the record

Changing a name or plot field in a PDF does not update an official Jamabandi record. Prepare a correction request and supporting evidence; the authority handles the record correction through its process.

This site is independent of the government. The editor does not determine acceptance, deadlines, stamp requirements or attestation.

Check the information before typing

Match the fields in the official format against original evidence. Verify names, village, account/plot references and the area unit without guessing. Fill only the information required for your case.

Preserve supporting records as issued. Merge them only when the portal asks for one combined file; keep them separate when separate uploads are requested.

Fictional practice example only

Example: “Applicant: Test Kumar; subject: request to reconcile name spelling; reference: supporting proof attached.” This is typing practice, not an affidavit format or evidence of land rights.

Check current instructions for affidavit wording, verification, signatures and attachments separately.

Final file review

Reopen the downloaded copy and check names, dates, vowel marks, conjuncts and every page. Web editing exports image-based pages; searchable text, selectability and preservation of digital signatures are not promised.
