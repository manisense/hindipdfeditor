How to Translate Hindi PDF to English Online Without Losing Page Formatting

A step-by-step guide to translating Hindi documents, circulars, and certificates into English (and vice versa) while keeping tables, headings, and margins intact.

Direct Answer: Translating Hindi PDFs with Layout Preservation

To translate a Hindi PDF to English without distorting page formatting, use Hindi PDF Editor Translate Tool. Open your document, select Hindi → English direction, click Translate, and the engine detects text bounding boxes and replaces the Hindi lines with translated English text in the exact original coordinates.

The Challenge with Traditional PDF Translation Tools

Most free PDF translators convert your document into raw text, pass it through an unformatted translator, and output a plain document with lost margins, broken columns, and vanished tables.

Hindi PDF Editor uses geometric in-place replacement:

  • Detects exact line coordinates and font metrics across the PDF page.
  • Processes translation through our secured Gemini AI proxy.
  • Renders translated text directly inside the original line bounding boxes.
  • Preserves page backgrounds, stamps, borders, and signatures.

Step-by-Step Translation Guide

Step 1: Open the Translate Tool

Go to https://hindipdfeditor.com/edit/?tool=translate and upload or drag your Hindi PDF.

Step 2: Choose Translation Direction

Choose between Hindi → English or English → Hindi from the top toolbar.

Step 3: Run Translation & Review

Click Translate Page. The translated text appears over the original lines with matched font sizes and styling. You can click any translated line to fine-tune the wording manually.

Step 4: Download the Translated PDF

Click Export PDF to download your freshly compiled translated document.
