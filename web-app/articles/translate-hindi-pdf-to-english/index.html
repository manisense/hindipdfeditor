Translate Hindi PDF to English: consent and review

Translate supported Hindi or English text through the AI service after consent. Review text accuracy, skipped lines and image-based PDF output.

Written by Manish · Reviewed 2026-10-01

Direct answer

Select a PDF, review the detected direction, explicitly consent to text and possible page-image processing, complete the security check and translate. Review the result; AI translation and layout can be wrong.

  • What is sent to the service
  • Limits and unsupported cases
  • Translation is not a certified document
  • Output and cancellation

What is sent to the service

Core editing runs in your browser. AI OCR and translation are separate options that send approved text or page images to the service after consent.

Detected source lines are used for translation. Difficult pages with incomplete text maps may use AI OCR on page images, as disclosed in the consent. Do not approve sensitive content unless this processing is appropriate for you.

Limits and unsupported cases

The UI currently limits source selection to 40 MB and 40 pages; service-side quotas may be lower and are enforced separately. Inspect current limits in the tool. Detected legacy fonts or uncertain inspection may block editing or require an OCR path according to the applicable safety policy.

Translation is not a certified document

Names, dates, amounts and technical terms need manual checking. Translated words can be longer than the original; boxes may wrap or overflow. Review skipped-line information and each page before use.

Output and cancellation

Reopen the downloaded copy and check names, dates, vowel marks, conjuncts and every page. Web editing exports image-based pages; searchable text, selectability and preservation of digital signatures are not promised.

Cancel stops the current workflow. A security or service error must be visible; it must not be treated as a successful translation.

Frequently asked questions

Is translation entirely on my device?

No. Approved content is sent to the AI service after consent.

Is the translated PDF official or certified?

No. Review accuracy and obtain any certification required by the receiving authority.

Work on your PDF

Core editing runs in your browser. AI OCR and translation send approved content for processing. Review the exported copy; the original PDF remains unchanged.
