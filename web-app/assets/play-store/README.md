# Play Store and web marketing assets

These are the current Google Play Store marketing creatives supplied for Hindi PDF Editor.

The five semantically named Android phone images are used in the lazy-loaded website showcase and
in `SoftwareApplication` structured data. `hindi-pdf-editor-tablet.png` is the website's large
Open Graph and Twitter sharing image. The remaining phone/tablet originals are retained here for
future Play Store listing and campaign work; they are deployed as static files but are not fetched
by the website during a normal page view.
