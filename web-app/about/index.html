About Hindi PDF Editor

A free tool for one stubborn problem: changing Hindi text in a PDF without the matras falling apart.

Why it exists

Anyone who has tried to fix a name on a Hindi form in a general PDF editor has seen it happen: the short-i matra jumps to the wrong side, "श्री" splits into pieces, and the page ends up looking worse than before. Most PDF tools were built for English and edit Hindi text without the shaping step Devanagari needs. Hindi PDF Editor was built to do that one job properly, for free, on a phone or a computer.

How it works

The editor does not try to rewrite the glyphs inside your PDF. Each page is shown as an image, and any text you add or replace is laid out by the browser's own text engine, the same one that displays Hindi web pages, using the Noto Sans or Noto Serif Devanagari font. That is why conjuncts like क्ष and matras like ि come out joined. On the web, each edited page is exported as a high-resolution image inside a new PDF. It looks the same everywhere, but that text cannot be selected or searched.

PDFs typed in old fonts such as Kruti Dev are detected when you open them. The web editor switches editing off on those pages rather than guessing. The Android app offers an opt-in mode that adds new Unicode text over such pages. Neither converts the old text.

What happens to your files

  • Opening, editing, merging, splitting and compressing run on your device. Your PDF is not uploaded.
  • Two optional features use a server, and only after you confirm: translation sends the detected text lines, and AI text recognition sends page images, to our Gemini-based service.
  • Your original file is never changed. Every export is a new file.

Details are in the privacy policy and data safety page.

What it is not for

It is for documents you are allowed to change: blank forms, affidavits, applications and your own drafts. Please don't use it to alter documents issued by an authority, such as admit cards, mark sheets or land records. Corrections to those come from the office that issued them, and our guides explain how.

Who makes it

Hindi PDF Editor is made by Manisense. It is free, with no account and no watermark, on the web and as an Android app. The source code is public on GitHub.
