About Hindi PDF Editor

A free tool for one stubborn problem: changing Hindi text in a PDF without the matras falling apart.

Why it exists

Anyone who has tried to fix a name on a Hindi form in a general PDF editor has seen it happen: the short-i matra jumps to the wrong side, "श्री" splits into pieces, and the page ends up looking worse than before. Most PDF tools were built for English and edit Hindi text without the shaping step Devanagari needs. Hindi PDF Editor was built to do that one job properly, for free, on a phone or a computer.

How it works

The editor does not try to rewrite the glyphs inside your PDF. Each page is shown as an image, and any text you add or replace is laid out by the browser's own text engine, the same one that displays Hindi web pages, using the Noto Sans or Noto Serif Devanagari font. That is why conjuncts like क्ष and matras like ि come out joined. On the web, the export starts from your original file: pages you did not edit are copied unchanged, and on edited pages the replaced text is removed and the new words are added as images. The rest of the text stays selectable, but the new words cannot be searched, and any edit invalidates a digital signature.

PDFs typed in old fonts such as Kruti Dev are detected when you open them. On the web, Kruti Dev and DevLys pages are converted to Unicode, checked against text recognition, and you review every line before editing. Other old fonts, such as Chanakya, and unknown encodings stay blocked rather than guessed. The Android app offers an opt-in mode that adds new Unicode text over such pages without converting the old text.

What happens to your files

  • Opening, editing, merging, splitting and compressing run on your device. Your PDF is not uploaded.
  • Two optional features use a server, and only after you confirm: translation sends the detected text lines, and AI text recognition sends page images, to our Gemini-based service.
  • Your original file is never changed. Every export is a new file.

Details are in the privacy policy and data safety page.

What it is not for

It is for documents you are allowed to change: blank forms, affidavits, applications and your own drafts. Please don't use it to alter documents issued by an authority, such as admit cards, mark sheets or land records. Corrections to those come from the office that issued them, and our guides explain how.

Who makes it

Hindi PDF Editor is built and maintained by Manish, a software engineer at Pruning Labs (LinkedIn). He also writes and reviews the guides on this site. It is free, with no account and no watermark, on the web and as an Android app. The source code is public on GitHub.
